# Documents used (public, official sources)

Download these into a `docs/` folder before running `python ingest.py`. The PDFs are not stored in this repository.

1. CISA, NSA, FBI, MS-ISAC. *Phishing Guidance: Stopping the Attack Cycle at Phase One* (October 2023).
   https://media.defense.gov/2023/Oct/18/2003322402/-1/-1/1/CSI-PHISHING-GUIDANCE.PDF
2. CISA. *Capacity Enhancement Guide: Counter-Phishing Recommendations for Non-Federal Organizations* (2020).
   https://www.cisa.gov/sites/default/files/publications/Capacity_Enhancement_Guide-Counter-Phishing-Recommendations_for_Non-Federal_Organizations.pdf
3. CERT-In (India). *Safe Internet Best Practices Booklet* (2024).
   https://www.csk.gov.in/documents/CERT-In_Safe_Internet-Best_Practices_Booklet.pdf
4. NCSC (UK). *Small organisations guide to cyber security* (published 9 April 2026). Four pages saved as PDF via the browser: Secure your email, Secure your important online accounts, Spotting cyber attacks, Protecting your devices.
   https://www.ncsc.gov.uk/collection/small-organisations-guide-to-cyber-security
